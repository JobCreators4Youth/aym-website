Mattru Jong is A Hidden Gem for Ecotourism and Economic Opportunities

By Ishani Kanapathipillai2 min read

Nestled along the scenic Jong River, Mattru Jong, the capital of Bonthe District in Sierra Leone, is an emerging destination that promises unique ecotourism experiences and significant economic potential. This vibrant town, steeped in history and natural beauty, is an ideal spot for local and international tourists looking to explore new horizons.

Ecotourism in Mattru Jong

Mattru Jong offers rich opportunities for ecotourism. The town is surrounded by lush landscapes and the tranquil Jong River, perfect for river tours and wildlife watching. The nearby Gambia-Mattru Oil Palm Plantation provides a fascinating glimpse into sustainable agricultural practices. Managed by Sierra Organic Palm, this plantation spans over 7,000 acres and highlights efforts to balance economic growth with environmental stewardship.

Additionally, Mattru Jong is close to Tiwai Island Wildlife Sanctuary, home to diverse wildlife, including primates and rare bird species. Ecotourists can engage in guided tours and nature walks, making the region a haven for biodiversity enthusiasts.

Cultural and Historical Significance

Mattru Jong is about natural beauty and is rich in cultural and historical significance. The town is home to the Mattru Hospital, founded in 1950, which has grown to become a cornerstone of healthcare in the region. The town’s historical roots are deep, with landmarks like the Mongerewa Jong Chiefdom offering a window into the traditional governance and heritage of the Mende people.

Infrastructure and Economic Development

Recent infrastructural developments are set to transform Mattru Jong into a more accessible and economically vibrant area. President Julius Maada Bio is spearheading the construction of the Mattru Jong-Senehun Bridge, which aims to replace the old manual ferry crossing and significantly enhance connectivity within the region. This bridge will facilitate the transport of agricultural products and boost local markets, creating new economic opportunities for residents.

Moreover, the Sierra Leone Opportunities Industrialization Center (SLOIC) in Mattru Jong actively develops skills. Funded by the World Bank, SLOIC provides vocational training in various fields, including information technology and agriculture, empowering the local youth and fostering economic growth.

Why Visit Mattru Jong?

  1. Natural Beauty: Enjoy the serene Jong River, lush plantations, and nearby wildlife sanctuaries.
  2. Cultural Heritage: Explore the historical sites and learn about the rich cultural heritage of the Mende people.
  3. Economic Opportunities: Witness the transformation through new infrastructural projects and vocational training centers.
  4. Adventure and Relaxation: Engage in ecotourism activities or relax in the peaceful environment of this hidden gem.

Mattru Jong is more than just a town; it’s a gateway to discovering Sierra Leone’s authentic beauty and potential. Whether you are a nature lover, a history buff, or seeking new investment opportunities, Mattru Jong has something to offer. Plan your visit today and be part of its exciting journey towards sustainable development and cultural preservation.
