Opportunity Sector

Enterprise & Youth Employment

We report on young entrepreneurs who identify problems, test ideas, serve customers and create value. AYM helps approved teams organize, assess feasibility and mobilize resources responsibly; funding and success are not guaranteed.

Strategic Framework

Empowerment Through Entrepreneurship

We walk with young founders from an idea to a working venture—training, mentorship, seed support and market connections.

Embarking on the Journey

  • Idea development and business basics.
  • Hands-on mentorship from real operators.
  • Seed support to launch a pilot enterprise.
  • Access to markets and a peer founder network.
