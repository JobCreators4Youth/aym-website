Primary Operational Hub: Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone is the global center of AYM and Mattru Jong is our operational base. Through the Global Learning and Sharing Endeavor (GLASE), the Mattru Jong Project brings together diverse global youth thinkers to implement educational centers, provide supplementary learning, and promote social change through youth entrepreneurship.

Currently powered by seven dedicated employees (projected to reach 25), operations are governed by the Global Assembly of Founders Circle, replacing the former Executive Board. (Note: Renovation of the center resumed after being paused during the Ebola outbreak).

Mattru Jong Project

A center for jobs, learning and local enterprise

AYM’s Sierra Leone page describes a Social Enterprise Center and Business Incubator in Mattru Jong, developed with community needs and youth opportunity in mind.

Examples highlighted on the page include phone and vehicle battery charging, electricity services, community food sales, vegetable gardens and a small sheep-rearing initiative. The broader Mattru Jong vision brings together employment, entrepreneurship, education and social change.

Community priorities

Jobs & local business

Create employment, strengthen the local business climate and open space for youth-led micro-enterprises.

Agriculture & education

Support local food production and supplementary education for learners from early childhood through adulthood.

Culture & participation

Preserve Jong Chiefdom landmarks, grow creative and recreational projects, and include local people and young leaders in development.
