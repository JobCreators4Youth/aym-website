Privacy Policy

How AYM handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

The Twenty-First Century African Youth Movement, Inc. (AYM) respects your privacy. This summary explains what information this website may receive, how it is used and how you can ask questions about it.

Information we receive

  • Information you provide: details you enter in contact, volunteer, story submission and other forms, such as your name, email, location, submitted text and consent choices.
  • Story submissions: drafts sent through Share Your Story are treated as review materials, not published content.
  • Browser storage: some features may use local browser storage to keep draft text or interface preferences on your device.
  • Service data: form and hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and review forms you submit.
  • To verify story submissions and assess safeguarding permissions.
  • To operate approved programs and keep records of consent where needed.
  • To improve the website and protect it from misuse.
  • To meet legal or governance requirements where applicable.

Sharing and service providers

AYM does not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate this site, such as form delivery, email, hosting and payment providers. Information may also be shared where required by law or where needed to protect people and property.

Stories and publication

Submitting a story through this website does not automatically publish it and does not grant AYM permission to publish. If a submission moves forward, AYM asks for separate, clear approval about how the story may be used. You may withdraw permission by contacting us, subject to material already published or shared under prior approval.

Children and safeguarding

We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. The story form asks submitters to confirm their age or guardian permission and to confirm they have the right to share details about other people.

Retention and your choices

We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, unless a longer period is required for legal, safety or recordkeeping reasons. You may ask to access, correct or delete information you submitted by contacting info@aym-inc.org.

Updates

We may update this page as our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@aym-inc.org or through our contact page. See also our Terms of Use and Accessibility pages.
