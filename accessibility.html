Accessibility

Our approach to making this website easier to use for more people.

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

AYM wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, magnification, captions or reduced motion settings.

Our approach

We aim to meet recognized web accessibility guidance, including WCAG 2.1 AA where practical. Accessibility is reviewed as pages are added and updated, rather than treated as a one-time task.

What we work on

  • Clear page structure, headings and descriptive link text.
  • Keyboard access to navigation, forms and interactive controls.
  • Visible focus styles and labels for form fields.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Readable text, sufficient contrast and responsive layouts.
  • Reduced motion options for animation and transitions where supported.
  • Status messages for form outcomes where supported.

Known limitations

Some media, archived articles or embedded third-party content may not yet meet the same standard. If you find something difficult to use, we want to know so we can improve it.

Give feedback

If a page, form or feature is hard to access, email info@aym-inc.org with the page address and a short description of the issue. You can also use our contact page.
