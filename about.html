VISION

An Africa where every young person has the voice, knowledge, skills, and economic opportunities to shape their future and contribute to the sustainable development of their community and continent.

MISSION

To empower African youth through digital media, education, entrepreneurship, civic engagement, and global collaboration by amplifying their voices, developing their skills, connecting them to opportunities, and transforming their ideas into sustainable economic and social impact.

VOTE Africa

Voices of the Emerging Africa

VOTE Africa is a global media and engagement initiative of the Twenty-First Century African Youth Movement, Inc. (AYM), a U.S.-registered nonprofit organization. It shares African voices, perspectives and practical information about opportunity sectors across the continent.

VOTE Africa is not a separate nonprofit, political party, fundraising vehicle or employer. Stories are published for learning and dialogue. Programs, grants and enterprise activity take place only under approved AYM governance and written authorization.

Our Story

The History and Story of AYM

Abu-Hassan Koroma migrated to the United States in 1991, immediately after the start of the Sierra Leone Civil War. The war (1991–2002) left an estimated 60% of young people economically and socially abandoned. At the end of the war, training alone was not enough—creating long-term alternatives for self-reliance required a broader coalition and practical pathways to work and enterprise.

Born out of the tragedy of a decade-long civil war that displaced and abandoned an entire generation of youth.

Founded in 2000 in Wisconsin, US to give rural Sierra Leonean youth a path to self-reliance.

From 7 staff to a growing team, AYM created enterprises, trained youth, and built the Mattru Jong project.

A growing Pan-African network of chapters across West, East, and Southern Africa.
