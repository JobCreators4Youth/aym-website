Get Involved

Share Your Story.

Send an experience, idea or community project to AYM for review. Nothing submitted here is published automatically.

Before You Begin

Your words stay in review until permission is clear.

This form sends a draft to the AYM team for editorial and safeguarding review. It is not a publishing tool, and submitting it does not grant permission to publish.

  • We review submissions for accuracy, context and safeguarding.
  • We may contact you to verify details or ask follow-up questions.
  • Publication is considered only after separate, clear approval.
Submission Form

Tell us your story

Use the fields below to share the basic details. You can write in your own words and do not need to include private or sensitive information.

Safeguarding consent *

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before submitting.

What happens next

1. Review

The team checks the submission for clarity, accuracy, safeguarding and fit with AYM's work.

2. Verification

We may ask questions, request context or check permissions before considering any story further.

3. Separate permission

If a story moves forward, AYM asks for clear approval about how it may be used. No approval is assumed from this form.

Prefer to talk first?

You can ask a question before sending a draft through our contact page.
