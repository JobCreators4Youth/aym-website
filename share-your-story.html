SHARE YOUR STORY

Before you submit

  • Share only information you have the right to share.
  • Do not include passwords, identity numbers, medical records, or other sensitive personal information.
  • If your story includes other people, make sure you have their permission.
  • Submitting the questionnaire does not automatically publish your story.

What happens next

1. Review

The AYM/VOE Africa team reviews submissions for clarity, accuracy, safeguarding, and fit with the movement?s mission.

2. Verification

We may contact you to verify details, request context, or discuss how your story could be shared responsibly.

3. Separate permission

If your story moves forward, AYM will request separate, clear approval before publication, distribution, or other use.

Publishing framework

The initial questionnaire supports content acquisition, global talent identification, and opportunity development. Before publication, AYM will integrate the Media Release Agreement, Editorial Standards, and safeguarding procedures. The Media Release Agreement will address editing, international distribution, copyright, platform monetization, compensation arrangements, and the use of a contributor?s name, image, and voice.

Need help?

Use the WhatsApp button, AYM Assistant, or our contact page if you have questions before submitting your story.
