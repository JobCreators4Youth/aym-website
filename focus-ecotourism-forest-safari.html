Eco-Tourism / Forest Safari

A Journey Into the Wild

Walk Sierra Leone's protected forests with a local guide and see the wildlife, rivers and canopy that make the country's interior so extraordinary.

Book Your Experience Ask a Question
The Experience

What you'll do in the forest

Guided forest walk

A knowledgeable local guide leads you along established trails through primary and secondary forest.

Wildlife & birdwatching

Spot monkeys, forest birds and plant life that exist nowhere else on earth.

Rivers & clearings

Rest at streams and forest clearings, with time for photos and questions along the way.

Good to know

What's included

  • A professional local guide for the full walk.
  • Food and drinks, including a traditional African dish.
  • Booking, planning and on-the-ground support.
  • Not included: transport to and from the trailhead.
  • Wear closed shoes and bring water and sun protection.
  • Tours run in small groups so the forest stays undisturbed.
