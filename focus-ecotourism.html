Featured Destination

Sustainable Travel & Community Development

Moselle Beach is in Mattru Jong, Sierra Leone. AYM is developing the area as an ecotourism destination and sharing the opportunity with visitors from within and beyond the country.

The ecotourism approach aims to create meaningful experiences while contributing to community well-being, environmental care and respect for local culture.
