Our Objectives

Change-makers rebuilding rural communities

AYM engages youth as change-makers in rebuilding and developing rural communities through innovative and progressive local-driven programs and enterprises. AYM facilitates increased access to critical resources and opportunities for rural youth.

1

Extinguish rural poverty

Help people acquire the right skills that will help them improve their livelihood.

2

Skill Development

Provide youth with job training to acquire employable skills valuable for their future.

3

Youth Empowerment

Shrink the share of youth most at risk of being left behind in the labor market.

4

African Intervention

Promote youth self-employment in Sierra Leone as a model for our African intervention.
