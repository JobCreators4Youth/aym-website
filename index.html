Who we are

Young people are the power behind a thriving Africa.

The Twenty-First Century African Youth Movement (AYM) is a youth-serving organization working alongside unemployed and underemployed young people to build stronger communities.

Through practical learning, enterprise, and community-led programs, we help young people turn their ideas and skills into opportunity, jobs, and lasting local progress.
