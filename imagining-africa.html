Imagining Africa

Building artistic bridges between Africa and America through imagination, creative exchange and fresh perspectives.

Explore AYM ArtsGet in touch
The initiative

Art can open a bridge between worlds.

Conceived by Abu-Hassan (Askia) Koroma and sponsored by the Twenty-First Century African Youth Movement, Imagining Africa uses art to build communication between Africa and America.

Artists are invited to examine how they imagine Africa, learn from new perspectives, and create work that can spark curiosity and dialogue. By approaching global challenges with creativity and optimism, the initiative encourages fresh ideas and understanding across cultures.

Purpose

Creative imagination, shared understanding

The project brings people together to learn, create and see the world from a wider perspective.

Our vision

Cross-cultural understanding

Foster understanding across cultures and encourage innovative ways to approach shared challenges through artistic imagination.

Our mission

Art that connects

Use the arts to build bridges between Africa and America, inspiring personal growth and positive transformation in the wider community.

A four-part creative journey

From first impressions to lasting dialogue

The project framework moves from imagining Africa to making art informed by learning, exchange and reflection.

Phase I

Imagining

Artists reflect on their perceptions of Africa and translate their ideas into an artwork, supported by self-guided learning and shared resources.

Phase II

The Dream

Deeper exploration of Africa’s many cultures inspires artists to develop new work and build on their original ideas.

Phase III

The Journey

The project envisions artists traveling to Sierra Leone to meet, observe and exchange ideas with artists there.

Phase IV

The Vision

Artists reflect on what they have learned through further creative work, continued connection and a shared exhibition.
