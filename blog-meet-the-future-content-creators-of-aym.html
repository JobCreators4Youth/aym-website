Meet the Future Content Creators of AYM!

By Ishani Kanapathipillai2 min read

In the vibrant town of Mattru Jong, a group of young talents is making a name for themselves as the future content creators of the Twenty-First Century African Youth Movement (AYM). These youngsters use drones, Android, iPhones, and laptops to capture, edit, and upload videos to the AYM YouTube channel.

Their journey starts with a simple idea: to showcase the beauty, resilience, and culture of their town through the eyes of its youth. With their basic equipment, they’re on a mission to create videos that tell stories of hope, determination, and community.

Every day, they hit the streets of Mattru Jong with their drone, capturing stunning aerial views of their hometown. They use their Android and iPhone cameras to capture everyday scenes, like the bustling markets and joyful gatherings of friends and family. These moments become the heart of their videos, filled with authenticity.

But the real magic happens during the editing process. With their trusty laptops, they weave visuals, music, and narration together to bring their stories to life. These videos transport viewers to the heart of Mattru Jong, thanks to the creativity of these young creators.

Once their videos are ready, they upload them to the AYM YouTube channel, awaiting approval from the AYM admin. It’s a nerve-wracking moment filled with pride, knowing their stories are ready to inspire and educate.

So, what can you find on the AYM YouTube channel? A collection of captivating videos showcasing Mattru Jong, the resilience of its people, and the dreams of its youth. From breathtaking drone shots to heartwarming interviews, each video is a testament to the dedication of these young content creators.

But they don’t want to go on this journey alone. They invite you to join them, to become a part of the AYM family. Visit their YouTube channel, subscribe, and share the videos that resonate with you. By doing so, you support these future content creators and help share their stories with the world.

The Twenty-First Century African Youth Movement (AYM) Content-Creators-In-Training are on a mission to share the beauty of Africa and the potential of its youth through digital storytelling. Join them on this journey, and together, let’s celebrate the richness of Mattru Jong.
