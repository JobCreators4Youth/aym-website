A community-centered approach

Start by understanding local needs

AYM is not a healthcare provider. Its approach is to understand community priorities and collaborate with organizations and people equipped to address them.

1

Understand the needs

Conduct a comprehensive assessment of the community’s health challenges and priorities.

2

Work with stakeholders

Collaborate with relevant community and health partners to shape a response.

3

Identify practical support

Potential responses include funding initiatives, training workers and providing equipment or supplies.

4

Evaluate and adjust

Review the impact of the response and adapt it as community needs evolve.

Impact Goals

Practical solutions through partnership

By listening to communities and working with relevant stakeholders, AYM aims to support solutions that respond to local healthcare priorities.
