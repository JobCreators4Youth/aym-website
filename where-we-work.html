Sierra Leone & the United States

Our work is rooted in community programs in Sierra Leone and supported through coordination in the United States.

Sierra Leone

AYM identifies Sierra Leone as the movement’s global center and Mattru Jong as its operational base. Its page describes a social enterprise and business incubator, with goals that include youth jobs, local enterprise, agriculture, supplementary education, ecotourism, arts and community participation.

AYM in Sierra Leone

United States

AYM is incorporated in Wisconsin. Its US page describes a coordinating role for the movement abroad, resource mobilization for Sierra Leone, recruitment of online education volunteers, and support for new chapter leaders.
